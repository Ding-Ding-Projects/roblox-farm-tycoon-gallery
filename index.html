About this gallery

These images are original pixel captures from focused Edit and Play sessions. They show world-only content and are presented as development evidence, not as a claim that the game is complete or final.

The 14 interior records were visually inspected and intentionally remain marked as overview captures. Earlier interface, fixture, earned-state, purchase, and failure-state views are included for context. The full 45-image inventory is indexed in the public capture inventory, with dates and timezone offsets taken from validated receipts where available.
