About this gallery

These images are original pixel captures from a focused Play session. They show world-only content and are presented as development evidence, not as a claim that the game is complete or final.

The 14 interior records were visually inspected and intentionally remain marked as overview captures. The historical HUD views are included for context and are not part of the current interior set.
