About this gallery

These images are original pixel captures from focused Edit and Play sessions. They show world-only content and are presented as development evidence, not as a claim that the game is complete or final.

The 14 interior records were visually inspected and intentionally remain marked as overview captures. Earlier interface, fixture, earned-state, purchase, failure-state, and work-in-progress views are included for context. The full 84-image inventory is indexed in the public capture inventory, with dates and timezone offsets taken from validated receipts where available.

The latest September 22 observations include a bilingual enlarged HUD at 1264 by 830, earlier English HUD and settings states, and a provisionally furnished lounge. Smaller viewports, display scales, separate settings-panel overlap, durable persistence, full catalogue coverage and performance remain unverified.
