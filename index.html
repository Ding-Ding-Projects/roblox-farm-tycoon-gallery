About this gallery

These images are original pixel captures from focused Edit and Play sessions. They show world-only content and are presented as development evidence, not as a claim that the game is complete or final.

The 14 interior records were visually inspected and intentionally remain marked as overview captures. Earlier interface, fixture, earned-state, purchase, failure-state, and work-in-progress views are included for context. The full 122-image inventory is indexed in the public capture inventory, with dates and timezone offsets taken from validated receipts where available.

The latest September 23 Play observations show the later tutorial revision and the earlier two-pixel label-width diagnostic. The bilingual Farm-board status still truncates. Earlier provisional arrival furniture, supported restroom geometry and session-only Settings views remain in the gallery. Camera-only room views do not prove fixture use, seating, walking clearance, final accessibility or accepted realism. Smaller viewports, display scales, durable persistence, full catalogue coverage and performance remain unverified.
