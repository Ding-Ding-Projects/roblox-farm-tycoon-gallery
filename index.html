About this gallery

These images are original pixel captures from focused Edit and Play sessions. They show world-only content and are presented as development evidence, not as a claim that the game is complete or final.

The 14 interior records were visually inspected and intentionally remain marked as overview captures. The two historical HUD views and one earlier barn interior are included for context. The 12 new evidence views are indexed in the public capture inventory, with dates and timezone offsets taken from validated receipts.
